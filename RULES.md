# Dragonhold: Rules Cheat Sheet

**Goal:** be the first house to reach **12 Renown ★**.

## Renown ★
| Source | ★ |
|---|---|
| Hamlet | 1 |
| Keep (upgraded Hamlet) | 2 |
| Wyrmling / Grown dragon | 1 / 2 |
| Great Wall: 5+ connected walls, and the longest | 2 |
| Mightiest Host: 3+ Sellswords played, and the most | 2 |
| Renown Scroll (kept secret) | 1 each |

## Start: landing
- The whole island is shown **only now**, on the war map.
- Houses take turns in snake order (1-2-3-4-4-3-2-1). Each places a **Hamlet + Wall**, twice.
- Your **2nd Hamlet** gives you starting resources. Then the **mist** hides what you haven't explored.

## Harvests
- They start fast (first after 8 s, then 12–20 s) and slow to **30–60 s**. The Rune Stones are cast **twice** each harvest.
- Regions showing the cast number produce: **Hamlet 1, Keep 2** (per region corner you own).
- **7 = Reaver Raid:** anyone holding **more than 7** cards loses half. The Horn-Bearer (rotates each harvest) moves the Reavers: their region produces nothing, and they rob 1 card from a rival there.

## Costs (supply limit)
| Build | Cost | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Wall | 🌲🪨 | 15 |
| Hamlet | 🌲🪨🌾🐄 | 5 |
| Keep (upgrade) | 🌾🌾🔥🔥🔥 | 4 |
| Lore Scroll | 🌾🐄🔥 | 25 in the library |
| Dragon Roost (beside a Keep) | 🪨🪨🔥🔥🌲 | 1 |
| Scorpion Tower | 🪨🪨🌲🔥 | 3 |

**Placement:** Hamlets go on corners, never next to another settlement, and must touch your wall. Walls extend your walls or settlements.

**🧱 Walls protect you:** rival lords can't cross your walls. You cross your own walls through their 🚪 **gate** (in the middle of each wall; the doors open for you) or right beside your own Hamlet/Keep at the wall's ends. Rivals may pass through **your** gate only by **paying you 1 resource** (a toll; the gate stays open for them ~12 s). You pay the same to pass theirs. Nobody can challenge you to a duel or raid your buildings through your wall. Dragons fly over walls.

## Trading
- **Guild:** 4 of one kind → 1 of anything (**3:1 at dusk**).
- **Ravens:** offer any bundle to the AI houses. They accept fair deals that help them, and refuse if you lead by 3+.

## Lore Scrolls
- You can play a scroll from the **next harvest** on, **one per harvest**.
- **Sellsword** (move the Reavers + rob) · **Renown Scroll** (+1★ secret) · **Wall Builders** (2 free walls) · **Bountiful Harvest** (take 2) · **Royal Decree** (claim everyone's stock of one resource)

## Dragons
1. Build a **Roost** beside a Keep.
2. Fetch an **egg** from the volcano and carry it home.
3. The egg eats 🔥 at 3 harvests → **Wyrmling** (+1★, ride it).
4. It eats 🐄🐄 at 3 harvests → **Grown dragon** (+2★).

- **Grown dragon:** 🔥 **Dragonfire** scorches a rival region so it produces nothing next harvest (recharges after 3 harvests). The **Dragon's Eye** lifts the mist.
- **Upkeep:** 🐄🔥 every 3rd harvest. If you can't pay, the dragon turns **Restless**: no riding, fire or Eye until fed.
- **Scorpion Tower:** rival dragons can't scorch regions touching it.

## Duels ⚔️
- Challenge a nearby lord, or get challenged. AI lords block your path more when you lead, carry 5+ cards, or walk on their land.
- **Best of 3:** Strike > Feint > Block > Strike. A tie goes to the mightier lord.
- **Might** = 1 + Sellswords played + fighting on your own land + grown dragon.
- **Winner** takes 2 cards (3 if 2+ mightier). The loser is knocked back. **Yield** = pay 1.

## Time of day (8-minute day)
- 🌅 **Dawn bounty:** the first harvest after dawn gives +1 per producing building.
- 🌇 **Dusk market** (~50 s): Guild 3:1, and the houses take any fair deal.
- 🌙 **Night raids** (~1.5 min): steal 1 card from a rival Hamlet or Keep, once per building per night.
  - Owner's lord within ~18 m → **duel**.
  - Owner's Scorpion Tower there → **raid fails**.

## Controls
- **Drag** to look · **click the ground or the map** to walk · **click a gold sign** to build
- **Build bar** on the left · **bottom buttons**: Trade, Scrolls, Dragon, Map, Home
- **Keys:** WASD / arrows to walk · 🎮 **D-pad** on touch screens · 👁️ **View** switches the camera
