How to Play

Renown

First house to 12 wins. Hamlet 1, Keep 2. Great Wall +2 for five or more joined walls. Mightiest Host +2 for three or more Sellswords.

Harvests

The Rune Stones are cast at each harvest. Tiles showing that number yield: 1 per Hamlet, 2 per Keep. A 7 wakes the Wyrm, and nothing is harvested where it sleeps.

Costs

Wall: 1 Ashwood, 1 Gravestone. Hamlet: 1 each of Ashwood, Gravestone, Blackwheat, Moth-silk. Keep: 2 Blackwheat, 3 Iron. Roost: [COST]. Scorpion Tower: [COST].

Trade

Guild trade is 4 for 1, or 3 for 1 at dusk. Send a raven to offer a trade to the other houses. Passing a rival's gate costs 1 resource.

Scrolls

Study a scroll to draw one of five: Sellsword, Renown Scroll, Wall Builders, Bountiful Harvest, Royal Decree.

Dragons

Carry an egg from the crater to a Roost beside your Keep. It hatches a wyrmling, then a grown dragon with Dragonfire and Dragon's Eye. A Scorpion Tower blocks Dragonfire and night raids nearby.

Duels

Challenge a rival rider. Best of three: Strike, Block or Feint. Yield by paying 1 resource.

Time of day

Dawn brings a bountiful harvest. Dusk opens the dusk market. Night is when raids happen.
