HOW TO PLAY

From the war table pick where to found 2 Hamlets with a road each, for free. This is your only look at the whole isle: once you land, mist hides everything you haven't explored.
Every 30–60 seconds (never quite the same) the Harvest Horn sounds and the Rune Stones are cast twice. Regions whose banner shows that number produce for every Hamlet (1) or Keep (2) on their corners.
A 7 brings a Reaver Raid: hold more than 7 and you lose half.
Race up to 3 AI houses (they walk the isles too) to 12 Renown.

HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

Be the first house to reach 12 Renown ★. Hamlets give 1★, Keeps 2★, a dragon up to 2★, and the longest road or biggest army of Sellswords 2★ each.

HARVESTS

Every 30–60 s the Rune Stones are cast twice. Each region whose number comes up gives resources to every Hamlet (1) and Keep (2) on its corners. A 7 brings the Reavers.
